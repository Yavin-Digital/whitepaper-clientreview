Get Found on Your Own Street

Why Doorstep Local Builds One Neighborhood at a Time

Draft for Mike's review. Not approved for publishing. Founding offer (first 30 businesses) per Mike, Nov 8 2026; the live site still shows 20 spots until the pending update merges. Membership $29/mo; ads $25/mo members, $30/mo non-members.

Section 1The problem

Most local businesses are invisible in the one place that matters most: the street they serve.

It's not because they're bad at what they do. The places they show up online were never built for them. Look at a typical listing and you'll see the same three problems.

Your listing is a ghost town. It's a name and a phone number, with nothing that gives a neighbor a reason to choose you over the next result.

It's built for the whole internet. You're lined up next to every business in the country. The people on your street, the ones who could actually hire you this week, never see you.

You're easy to scroll past. A tiny logo and a generic profile, so neighbors move on before they ever call.

I've spent years building websites and marketing for small businesses, and this keeps coming up. The big platforms were built for search engines and shareholders. They weren't built for the person three streets over who needs a roofer, a tutor or a painter and would rather hire someone local.

That's the gap Doorstep Local was built to close.

Section 2The model: one neighborhood at a time

Doorstep Local doesn't try to be everywhere. That's the point.

Each neighborhood gets its own directory. When you list your business, you show up where the people who can hire you already live, not buried in a national index.

Right now that means 4 neighborhoods across 2 cities:

  • Northlake: Canyon Falls, Pecan Square and The Ridge at Northlake
  • Argyle: Harvest

We're building it this way on purpose. A thin listing everywhere gets you lost. Depth on one street gets you hired.

Section 3What a real profile looks like

A neighbor deciding who to call wants to see more than a phone number. They want to know who you are, what your work looks like and whether you're open.

Every Doorstep Local profile has room for:

  • Photos of your work
  • A video
  • Your hours
  • A real about section that tells your story

The goal is simple. A neighbor should see enough to call you with confidence, and they should see the work before they ever pick up the phone.

Section 4Advertising built for neighborhoods

Most ad platforms sell you reach. Reach into people who will never hire you is just spend.

Doorstep Local's ads run on the directories that serve your area, so the people seeing them are your actual neighbors. There are three formats:

  • Featured strip: the highest visibility on the directory
  • Grid card: a native format that sits alongside listings
  • Profile sidebar: shown to neighbors who are already looking at profiles

Members pay $25 per directory per month. Everyone else pays $30.

Section 5How it works

Getting set up takes three steps.

  1. Join your community. Choose the neighborhood directory that serves your area.
  2. Build your profile. Add photos, a video, your hours and a real about section.
  3. Get found and grow. Add more neighborhoods when you're ready, and advertise to your neighbors at the member rate.

If you'd rather see it than read it, there's a 2:22 explainer video on the homepage at doorsteplocal.com.

Section 6Simple, transparent, fair pricing

I've seen too many small business owners get caught in contracts, setup fees and pricing pages built to confuse. We didn't want any of that.

  • $29 a month for your directory listing once your free period ends
  • $25 a month for each extra neighborhood
  • No setup fees
  • No annual contracts
  • Cancel anytime

That's the whole pricing page.

Section 7Where it stands today

Doorstep Local is early. 7 local businesses are listed so far.

That's not a results story yet, and I'm not going to pretend it is. It's a starting point: real businesses in real neighborhoods building out their profiles. We'll share what we learn as the directories grow.

Section 8The founding offer

We're opening Doorstep Local with a founding member offer, and it's simple.

The first 30 businesses get it free for 90 days.

  • Your 90 free days start on launch day
  • No credit card. No spam.
  • After that, it's $29 a month, and you can cancel anytime

Once those 30 spots are gone, they're gone. If your customers live in Canyon Falls, Pecan Square, The Ridge at Northlake or Harvest, claim your spot at doorsteplocal.com before someone down the street does.
